# Kit 02 — Site etiquette & professionalism (v0.1.0)

## Before you arrive

- Confirm time, entrance, parking, dress code, and badging requirements in writing.
- Bring government ID if required; arrive early.
- Silence your phone; use it only if your host allows.

## On the floor

- Introduce yourself: name, school/program, that you are an **observer**.
- Stand where you are told; do not block doorways, crash carts, or workstations.
- Do not give clinical advice, translate medical decisions, or touch patients unless your program and site authorize hands-on activities (many shadowing programs are observation-only).
- If a patient or family is uncomfortable, step out immediately and thank them.

## Communication

- Use calm, respectful language.
- Save clinical questions for hallway debriefs, not mid-procedure.
- Never argue with staff in front of patients.

## After the session

- Thank your host.
- Complete any hour-verification steps your program requires.
- Reflect privately without PHI.
