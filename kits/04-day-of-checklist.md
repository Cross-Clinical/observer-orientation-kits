# Kit 04 — Day-of observer checklist (v0.1.0)

## Bring

- [ ] Photo ID / badge
- [ ] Closed-toe shoes, dress-code clothes
- [ ] Water bottle if allowed; light snack for break
- [ ] Notebook (no patient identifiers)
- [ ] Vaccination card / paperwork if requested

## Do

- [ ] Check in with volunteer/education desk or host
- [ ] Complete any day-of attestation
- [ ] Clarify observation-only vs hands-on boundaries
- [ ] Confirm pickup/end time

## Don’t

- [ ] Don’t take photos
- [ ] Don’t browse charts or workstations
- [ ] Don’t share encounters on social media
- [ ] Don’t diagnose, advise, or interpret results for patients

## Hour log reminder

Capture start/end times and specialty for your ledger (see `shadowing-hours-schema`). Host verifies later.
