# Kit 01 — HIPAA basics for observers (v0.1.0)

**Audience:** High school, college, and early-career observers  
**Purpose:** Educational primer — not legal advice. Follow your site’s privacy training.

## Core idea

If you can identify a person and learn something about their health or care, treat it as confidential. Do not share it outside the authorized care/education setting.

## Observer rules of thumb

1. **No stories outside the walls** — not to friends, social media, group chats, or class presentations with identifiable details.
2. **No photos or recordings** of patients, screens, whiteboards, labels, or documents unless the site explicitly authorizes a supervised educational activity.
3. **Minimum necessary** — only look/listen to what your host says you may observe for learning.
4. **Ask before accessing anything** — never touch EHR workstations, paper charts, or badge doors without instruction.
5. **Report mistakes immediately** — if you overhear something sensitive or make an error, tell your preceptor/coordinator the same day.

## Identifiers to avoid repeating

Names, faces, room numbers combined with dates, unique stories, school/employer details that pinpoint a person, photos of wristbands, etc.

## Reflection prompt (for journals)

Write what you *learned about a role or workflow* without including who the patient was or unique clinical details.
