# Kit 05 — Social media boundaries (v0.1.0)

## Default rule

**If it happened in a clinical setting, don’t post it** — even if you think it is anonymized. Unique stories are often re-identifiable.

## Never post

- Patient faces, voices, rooms, wristbands, monitors, whiteboards
- Staff gossip or complaints about named clinicians
- “Guess the diagnosis” games from real encounters
- Photos in scrubs that include unit signage + timestamps that locate care

## Safer alternatives

- Post about your career goals, study habits, or public events (HOSA competitions)
- Thank mentors only with their permission and without clinical details
- Share open educational resources and Cross Clinical OSS tools

## If you already posted something risky

1. Delete it.
2. Tell your program coordinator.
3. Complete any site-required incident steps.
